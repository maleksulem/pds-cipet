1. Outlier Detection via Interquartile Range (IQR)

Extreme spikes (e.g. sensor surge) and drops distort machine learning regression lines. The IQR method calculates statistical fences to isolate and cap anomalies.

First Quartile (Q1 - 25th percentile) -- kWh
Third Quartile (Q3 - 75th percentile) -- kWh
Interquartile Range (IQR = Q3 - Q1) -- kWh
Lower Bound (Q1 - 1.5 * IQR) -- kWh
Upper Bound (Q3 + 1.5 * IQR) -- kWh
Treatment Strategy Winsorization / Boundary Capping (Preserves record count without skewing weights)
2. Normalization & Feature Engineering

Demonstration of scaling formulas and cyclical engineered features:

Min-Max Scaling: x_scaled = (x - min) / (max - min) → Maps values to range [0, 1].
Z-Score Standardization: z = (x - mean) / std → Centers distribution at mean=0, std=1.
  • is_weekend: Binary flag (1 for Saturday & Sunday, 0 for weekdays).
  • is_peak_hour: Binary flag (1 for morning 8–11 & evening 18–21 demand spikes).
  • previous_consumption: Autoregressive 1-hour temporal lag feature.
