Interquartile Range (IQR) Outlier Capping

Extreme anomalous spikes caused by sensor malfunctions or storm disturbances distort linear model weights. We apply IQR Winsorization:

1. First Quartile (Q1, 25th percentile): 181.70 kWh
2. Third Quartile (Q3, 75th percentile): 237.20 kWh
3. Interquartile Range (IQR): 237.20 − 181.70 = 55.50 kWh
4. Upper Fence: Q3 + 1.5 × 55.50 = 320.45 kWh
5. Lower Fence: Q1 − 1.5 × 55.50 = 98.45 kWh
Rule: Any observed electrical load > 320.45 kWh is capped down to 320.45 kWh. Any value < 98.45 kWh is floored up to 98.45 kWh.
